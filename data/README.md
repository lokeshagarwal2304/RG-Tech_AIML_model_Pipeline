## 📂 Grain Species & Optical Datasets
1. **Rice (*Oryza sativa*):**
   - 24,767 high-resolution single-kernel optical images.
   - 8 defect categories (`0_NOR` through `7_IM`).
   - Split into train (17,342), val (2,472), and test (4,953) with 0.0% session leakage.

2. **Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*):**
   - Calibrated physical optical samples across major defect classes (Sound, Broken, Fusarium Scab, Sprouted, Pest, Moldy).
   - Enforces USDA GIPSA (FGIS) commercial grading standards.

3. **Sorghum (*Sorghum bicolor*):**
   - Calibrated spherical optical samples across defect classes.
   - Enforces Codex Alimentarius CXS 172-1989 standards.
